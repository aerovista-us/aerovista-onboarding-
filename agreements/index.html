Protected onboarding

Understand what you're agreeing to.

This public guide explains the document-signing process. It is not an agreement, an electronic signature, or proof of acceptance.

Integration status: AeroVista is preparing verified signing through ACOS and Documenso. Until activation, your administrator must provide any required documents through an approved process. Do not submit personal information or signatures on this public page.

Before access

  1. Sign in with your AeroCoreOS account.
  2. Confirm the role and organization represented.
  3. Review the complete applicable agreement, including any attachments.
  4. Sign using the approved electronic-signature process.
  5. Receive a signed copy and confirmation.

What gets recorded

  • Canonical AVCC identity and role
  • Exact document version and immutable digest
  • Signer consent and signature evidence
  • Timestamp and transaction identifier
  • Signed document receipt and audit trail

After signing: what happens?

  1. The signing provider independently confirms the completed document and signer.
  2. ACOS verifies the assigned agreement and approved document version.
  3. A signed PDF and audit record are retained in authorized storage, associated with the correct tenant and identity.
  4. Your onboarding status changes only after signed evidence and other required checks are verified.

The workflow above is planned; it is not active on this public page.

Different agreements for different relationships

Employees, contractors, collaborators, and business partners may need different confidentiality terms. Intellectual-property assignment, acceptable-use requirements, and employment or contractor contracts are separate instruments unless expressly included in a reviewed agreement.
