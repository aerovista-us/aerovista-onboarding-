Your privacy matters. This public site does not identify you or silently record personality scores, skills, or activity in AeroVista Identity. When secure account linking is enabled, we'll ask permission before saving selected interests or first-project preferences. Access rights, completed training, and demonstrated skills will be recorded separately.

What happens after a couple of visits?

  1. Explore: see what AeroVista builds, and follow what catches your attention.
  2. Try something real: choose one small, reversible contribution, ideally something you already understand.
  3. Talk about the result: what worked, what felt frustrating, and what you'd like to try next.
  4. Shape your path: a collaborator and AeroVista lead can agree on next opportunities or training—not assign a permanent label based on a quiz.
